New Product Direction: “Hooks” should become a core part of the Creative Rooms ecosystem.

Important:
Hooks are NOT just file uploads.

Hooks are:

* creative signals
* unfinished ideas
* emotional invitations
* spontaneous collaboration starters

This is a major conceptual difference.

NEW STRUCTURE:

1. HOOKS = ENTRY POINTS
   Hooks should become their own section/category inside the platform.

Hooks are small creative ideas people throw out into the world:

* guitar riffs
* melodies
* chorus ideas
* piano loops
* lyric concepts
* textures
* unfinished emotional moments

The feeling should be:
“Does anyone hear something in this?”

2. HOOK FLOW
   Users should be able to:

* click “Drop a Hook”
* upload a short audio snippet
* add a short emotional description
* choose vibe tags
* select what they are looking for

Examples:

* vocals needed
* drums wanted
* lyrics needed
* looking for collaborators

Hooks should feel:

* immediate
* low pressure
* spontaneous
* emotional
* human

NOT:

* polished content uploads
* social media posts
* finished songs

3. HOOKS CREATE ROOMS
   This is important:

When people join a hook,
a real Creative Room should naturally form around it.

Flow:
Drop Hook
→ people join
→ room forms
→ collaboration begins

Hooks are the beginning of the creative journey.

Creative Rooms are the deeper collaboration space.

4. SMALL INTIMATE SESSIONS
   Hooks should create:

* small rooms
* intimate collaboration
* emotional creative energy

Recommended:
2–4 people max per hook room.

When full:
the room locks automatically.

This creates:

* focus
* intimacy
* creative chemistry
* emotional connection

5. HOOK FEED
   Build a dedicated Hooks discovery section.

Each hook should display:

* waveform preview
* creator name
* emotional description
* vibe tags
* open seats remaining

Examples:

🎸 “Late night guitar idea”
Looking for vocals
2 spots left

🎹 “Dreamy piano atmosphere”
Need drums + textures

The feed should feel:

* cinematic
* alive
* emotional
* like creative signals floating through the night

NOT:

* content feed
* TikTok
* SoundCloud clone

6. UX DIRECTION
   Hooks must remain extremely simple.

The user experience should feel:

* effortless
* inviting
* emotionally creative
* immediate

A user should be able to:
record or upload a hook
in under 20 seconds.

Avoid:

* complicated upload flows
* too many settings
* technical friction

7. OVERALL VISION
   Creative Rooms is becoming:
   a human creative signal network.

People are not just uploading music.
They are sending creative signals into the world
hoping someone resonates with them.

Protect that feeling throughout the UX and design.
