The landing page is finally starting to feel emotionally strong and believable.
The atmosphere now feels warm, cinematic and human — not just like an AI-generated interface.

But there are still a few important things holding it back from feeling truly premium and alive.

1. THE LOGO STILL NEEDS MORE PRESENCE

The logo is better now, but it still feels too small and weak compared to the emotional weight of the page.

Please refine the brand presence further:

* make the waveform symbol larger
* increase spacing and readability
* improve contrast against the background
* add a subtle warm glow or ambient light around it
* make it feel iconic and memorable

Right now it still feels like a navbar item instead of the identity of the platform.

The logo should instantly communicate:
“This is Creative Rooms.”

2. THE TOP AREA STILL FEELS SLIGHTLY EMPTY

The landing page composition is much stronger now,
but there is still a bit too much dead dark space around the top section.

Please add subtle cinematic atmosphere:

* soft ambient gradients
* tiny floating particles/dust
* faint animated waveform texture
* gentle warm light movement

Nothing flashy.
Just enough to make the page feel alive and immersive.

3. THE PLATFORM NEEDS TO FEEL ACTIVE IMMEDIATELY

The page looks beautiful now,
but users still need to instantly feel:
“Something is happening here right now.”

Please add subtle live-platform indicators like:

* “3 hooks waiting now”
* “1 spot left”
* “Someone needs vocals”
* “Live room”
* soft pulse animations
* tiny activity indicators

This is important because it transforms the platform from:
“a static website”
into:
“a living creative network”.

4. KEEP THE CURRENT HERO STRUCTURE

The current landing layout is finally working:

* headline placement works
* CTA buttons work
* image works
* emotional tone works

Please keep this direction.
Do not redesign it completely again.

Instead:
refine and elevate what already exists.

5. THE EXPERIENCE SHOULD FEEL HUMAN — NOT TECH

Very important:
the platform should never feel like:

* Discord
* Zoom
* Slack
* a productivity app
* a gaming UI

It should feel like:
a late-night creative studio on the internet.

Warm.
Real.
Human.
Intimate.
Creative.

That emotional feeling is now finally starting to appear — protect that direction carefully.
